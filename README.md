# Alisha's Birthday Magic

### 📋 COPY PROMPT — LOVABL

:::writing{variant="standard" id="74163" title="Lovable Prompt — Alisha Birthday Website"} START OF PROMPT

Create a stunning, romantic, interactive birthday surprise website for my girlfriend **Alisha**, made by **Jibran**.

This should NOT look like a generic birthday template. It should feel like a personal, magical and emotional birthday experience created especially for Alisha.

## ❤️ DESIGN & VIBE

Use a beautiful romantic theme with:

- Pink
- Rose red
- Soft purple
- Warm golden
- Dark romantic gradients

Use elegant typography, glassmorphism cards, glowing elements, soft shadows and beautiful gradients.

The website must be:

- Romantic
- Cute
- Emotional
- Magical
- Cinematic
- Premium-looking
- Fully responsive on mobile and desktop

Add beautiful animated:

- Hearts
- Rose petals
- Sparkles
- Stars
- Glowing particles
- Confetti
- Fireworks

Animations should be smooth and elegant.

---

## 🌙 OPENING SCREEN

Create a full-screen cinematic opening.

Initially show:

"Hey Alisha ❤️"

Then animate the following text:

"Someone very special has a birthday today..."

After a few seconds reveal:

"Happy Birthday, My Love, Alisha 🎂❤️"

Then:

"With all my love — Jibran ❤️"

Add a glowing animated button:

"Open Your Surprise ❤️"

When Alisha clicks the button, transition beautifully into the main experience with hearts, sparkles and a smooth cinematic animation.

---

## 🎂 ANIMATED BIRTHDAY CAKE

Create a beautiful animated birthday cake in the center of the screen.

Cake design:

- Pink and white frosting
- Multiple layers
- Cute decorations
- Glowing candles
- Realistic animated candle flames
- Small floating sparkles around the cake

Above the cake write:

"Make a Wish, Alisha ✨"

Allow Alisha to interact with the candles.

When she taps/clicks the candles, extinguish the candle flames with a beautiful animation.

After all candles are extinguished:

- Trigger confetti
- Trigger heart particles
- Trigger sparkles
- Show a celebration animation
- Make the screen glow

Then reveal:

"Happy Birthday Alisha! 🎂❤️"

And underneath:

"May every wish in your heart come true. ✨"

---

## 💖 LOVE MESSAGE SECTION

Create a beautiful romantic section titled:

"Why You're So Special To Me ❤️"

Reveal each message one by one with elegant scroll animations.

Show messages such as:

"Alisha, you make ordinary moments feel magical. ❤️"

"Your smile is one of my favorite things in this world. ✨"

"Every moment with you becomes a memory I want to keep forever. 🌹"

"You have a way of making my world brighter just by being in it. 💕"

"And if I could choose again, I'd still choose you. ❤️"

At the end show:

"Forever yours, Jibran ❤️"

---

## 💌 INTERACTIVE LOVE LETTER

Create a beautiful animated envelope.

On the envelope write:

"Open this letter, Alisha 💌"

When she clicks the envelope:

- Envelope opens with a smooth animation
- A letter comes out
- Background becomes softer and more romantic
- Add floating hearts around the letter

The letter should say:

"Dear Alisha,

Today is your special day, but honestly, I feel like I'm the lucky one because I get to have someone as beautiful and special as you in my life.

You bring happiness into my life in ways that words can't completely explain.

Your smile, your little moments, your presence — they all mean more to me than you probably realize.

I hope this birthday brings you as much happiness as you bring into my life.

I want you to know that you are deeply loved, genuinely appreciated, and incredibly special to me.

Happy Birthday, my love.

Keep smiling, keep shining, and always remember that there is someone who loves seeing you happy.

With all my heart,

Jibran ❤️"

Make the letter look handwritten and elegant.

---

## 📸 OUR MEMORIES

Create a beautiful section titled:

"Our Little World 📸❤️"

Create an elegant romantic photo gallery where I can easily replace placeholder images with my own photos of Alisha and Jibran.

Use beautiful animated photo cards.

Each photo should have:

- Rounded corners
- Soft glow
- Hover/tap animation
- Elegant caption

Example captions:

"Our beautiful moments ❤️"

"One of my favorite memories"

"You + Me ❤️"

"My favorite person"

"Forever grateful for you"

Make sure placeholder images never appear broken.

---

## ❤️ INTERACTIVE HEART

Create a section titled:

"How Much Love Can One Heart Hold? ❤️"

Put a large glowing animated heart in the center.

Allow Alisha to tap/click the heart.

Every click should:

- Create a heart burst
- Generate small floating hearts
- Create sparkles
- Slightly animate the big heart

After multiple clicks reveal:

"You've filled this whole page with love...

just like you've filled my heart. ❤️"

---

## 🎉 BIRTHDAY CELEBRATION

Create a special celebration section.

Display:

"Today is all about YOU, Alisha! 👑❤️"

Add:

- Animated confetti
- Fireworks
- Floating hearts
- Sparkles
- Glowing particles

Make this section feel like a huge birthday celebration.

---

## 🌹 FINAL ROMANTIC SURPRISE

Create a beautiful full-screen cinematic ending.

Use a dark romantic background with:

- Stars
- Glowing particles
- Floating hearts
- Soft pink/purple light

Reveal the text slowly, one line at a time:

"Alisha..."

"Thank you for being you. ❤️"

"Thank you for being part of my life."

"You are loved more than words can explain."

Then make the final message appear in large elegant typography:

"Happy Birthday, My Love ❤️"

Then:

"Forever & Always, Jibran ❤️"

Add a beautiful final button:

"Replay Our Story ✨"

Clicking it should smoothly take the user back to the beginning.

---

## 🎵 MUSIC

Add a romantic background music system.

Include a visible music button such as:

"🎵 Music On/Off"

Do not force autoplay if the browser blocks autoplay.

The music should be optional and easy to turn on/off.

---

## ✨ ANIMATION DETAILS

Use lots of tasteful animations throughout the website:

- Fade in
- Fade out
- Slide up
- Slide down
- Scale in
- Typewriter effects
- Text reveal
- Floating hearts
- Floating rose petals
- Sparkles
- Candle flame animation
- Cake reveal
- Envelope opening
- Heart burst
- Confetti
- Fireworks
- Glowing buttons
- Image hover animations
- Scroll-triggered animations
- Smooth transitions between sections

Make animations smooth and premium, not childish or excessive.

---

## 📱 MOBILE EXPERIENCE

The website must be designed mobile-first because Alisha will most likely open it on her phone.

Make sure:

- Text is readable
- Buttons are easy to tap
- Cake fits properly on mobile
- Photos look beautiful
- Animations don't cause horizontal scrolling
- Everything works with touch
- No elements overlap
- No broken layouts

---

## 🛠️ CODE QUALITY

Build this as a polished modern website.

Requirements:

- Clean reusable components
- Responsive layout
- Good performance
- Smooth animations
- No console errors
- No broken buttons
- No broken images
- No unnecessary complexity

Make it easy for me to later replace:

- Photos
- Background music
- Love letter
- Birthday messages

---

## ❤️ MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT

The final website should feel like:

"Jibran made this entire magical birthday experience specifically for Alisha."

It should make her feel:

- Loved
- Special
- Appreciated
- Emotional
- Surprised
- Happy

Do NOT make it look like a basic birthday website.

Make it feel like a **romantic digital love story + birthday surprise**.

Use the names **Alisha** and **Jibran** naturally throughout the experience.
